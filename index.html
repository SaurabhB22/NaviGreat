Getting here

Arriving on campus, from anywhere in Kanpur.

CSJMU sits right on the Kanpur Metro's Orange Line — two stations open directly onto the campus perimeter.

Vishwavidyalaya Metro Station

On the Orange Line, right beside the university. The closest stop for the Main Gate and the eastern campus.

Orange Line · Kanpur Metro

SPM Hospital Metro Station

The next stop over, also adjoining CSJMU — a useful alternative if you're headed for the western hostels.

Orange Line · Kanpur Metro

Main Gate, Kalyanpur

The primary vehicle entrance off the Kalyanpur road — start your NavGrade route from here if you're arriving by car or auto.

About this build

Built for CSJMU, from public data.

NavGrade is an independent student-tools concept, not an official University publication. The campus is genuinely centred on CSJMU's Kalyanpur coordinates (26.498°N, 80.266°E), and the two Kanpur Metro stations are real, verified stops on the Orange Line. Individual building pins, however, are placed illustratively until an official campus geodata set is available — treat distances as estimates, not survey-grade measurements.

Have accurate building coordinates or a campus layout to contribute? That's exactly the kind of data that would make the live map official.
